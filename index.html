SplitPeer

Shared expenses for groups.

Getting started

Create a group with the + button, then invite friends from Members: tap someone nearby, show the QR code, or share the invite link.

Add expenses and choose how to split them: equally, exact amounts, percentages or shares.

Settle up shows the fewest payments needed. Tap Pay to see someone's bank details, then record the payment once it's sent.

Common questions

Why aren't my friend's changes showing up?
Phones catch up when you're together with the app open, or through iCloud if it's turned on in your profile. Pull down on a group to check for updates. If you're not using iCloud, send a sync file from the group menu.

Someone in my group doesn't have the app yet.
Add them by name or from your contacts in Members. When they join, they can claim their spot and their balance moves to them.

Does SplitPeer move money?
No. It keeps track of who owes what and shows people's payment details. You pay each other however you like.

I got a new phone.
Each phone is its own member for now. Ask someone in the group to invite you again, and add yourself to new expenses from there.
