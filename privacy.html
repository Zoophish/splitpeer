SplitPeer

Privacy Policy

Last updated 12 October 2026

SplitPeer is an app for splitting expenses with friends. It's built so that your information stays with you and the people you share a group with. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the developer has no servers that receive your data.

What the app stores

Everything you enter is stored on your iPhone:

  • Your profile: your name, an emoji or photo, a colour, and any payment details you choose to add (such as your account name, sort code, account number, IBAN or a payment link).
  • Your groups, their members, expenses, payments and history.
  • A private key the app creates for you. It's kept in your iPhone's Keychain and never leaves your device.

Who can see it

Your profile and everything you add to a group are shared with the other members of that group, because that's how the group stays in sync. That includes your photo and payment details, if you've added them. Groups are shared in these ways:

  • Directly between phones nearby, over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth, while the app is open.
  • Through iCloud, if iCloud sync is turned on. Updates are encrypted on your phone using a key only your group's members have, then stored in SplitPeer's iCloud database. Neither the developer nor Apple can read them. Apple can see that encrypted data exists and an anonymous identifier for the iCloud account that stored it. You can turn iCloud sync off in your profile.
  • In sync files that you choose to send, for example by AirDrop or Messages. These are also encrypted with the group's key.

Anyone with a group's invite link or QR code can join that group and see its contents, so only share invites with people you mean to.

Contacts and photos

If you add someone from your contacts, or link a group member to a contact, the app only sees the one contact you pick. It keeps that person's name and photo on your phone to show next to them. It doesn't upload them or share them with your group. It never reads your whole address book and doesn't ask for permission to.

If you choose a profile photo, the app only sees the photo you pick. It's shrunk and shared with your groups as part of your profile.

What the developer collects

Nothing. SplitPeer doesn't collect, sell or share personal data. The app contains no analytics or advertising code. If you send feedback through TestFlight or by email, the developer only receives what you choose to send.

Deleting your data

Removing a group, or deleting the app, removes the data from your phone. Other members keep their own copies of groups they're in, and the group history includes earlier versions of each member's profile. Encrypted iCloud records stay in SplitPeer's iCloud database but can only be read by people who have the group's key.

Children

SplitPeer isn't aimed at children and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.
